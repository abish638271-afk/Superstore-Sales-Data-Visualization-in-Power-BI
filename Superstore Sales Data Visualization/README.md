# 📊 Data Visualization and Storytelling using Power BI

## Internship Task 2

**Internship Role:** Data Analyst Intern  
**Task:** Task 2 – Data Visualization and Storytelling  
**Tool Used:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

---

## Objective

The objective of this task is to create meaningful data visualizations that communicate business insights and tell a clear story using charts and dashboards.

The dashboard focuses on sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and sales trends.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Dataset

The project uses the **Superstore Sales Dataset**.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- **Technology** generated the highest category sales at approximately **243K**.
- The **West region** recorded the highest profit at approximately **19.8K** among the four regions.
- **September** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **81K**.
- **February** recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately **9K**.
- **Machines** generated the highest sales among the displayed sub-categories at approximately **106K**.
- **California** recorded approximately **82K** in sales among the displayed states.
- **New York** recorded the highest profit among the displayed states at approximately **21.4K**.

## Data Visualization Techniques

The dashboard uses different visualization types according to the nature of the data:

| Visualization | Purpose |
|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Display important business metrics |
| Line Chart | Analyze sales trends over time |
| Bar Chart | Compare products, regions and sub-categories |
| Column Chart | Compare category sales |
| Scatter Chart | Analyze discount and profit relationship |
| Slicers | Enable interactive filtering |

---

## Dashboard Design Principles

The dashboard was designed with the following principles:

- Keep the dashboard clean and easy to understand.
- Use appropriate charts for different types of data.
- Avoid unnecessary visual clutter.
- Highlight important business metrics.
- Use consistent formatting.
- Provide context and insights for the visualizations.
- Focus on business insights rather than visuals alone.
